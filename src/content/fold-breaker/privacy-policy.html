Privacy Policy — Fold Breaker

Last updated: 31 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Fold Breaker (the “App”), published by enidev (“we”, “us”), handles information when you use the App. By using the App you agree to this policy.

Summary

  • We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • Your game progress and settings are stored on your device.
  • The App shows ads through Google AdMob and offers optional purchases/subscriptions processed by Apple (with RevenueCat used to validate them).
  • These third parties may process device and advertising identifiers as described below.

Information processed

On-device data. Your level progress, coins, stickers, owned items and preferences are saved locally on your device. We do not collect or receive this data.

Advertising (Google AdMob). To show and measure ads, Google AdMob may process device information and advertising identifiers (such as the Identifier for Advertisers), approximate usage and general location derived from your IP address. On iOS, personalized advertising uses the advertising identifier only if you allow tracking in the App Tracking Transparency prompt; if you decline, ads are still shown but are not personalized. Google’s handling of this data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Purchases and subscriptions. In-app purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We use RevenueCat to validate receipts and manage your entitlement; RevenueCat receives a random app-generated identifier and your purchase history, but not your name or email. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy and Apple Privacy Policy.

How the information is used

  • To display and measure advertising and limit repeated or invalid ads.
  • To deliver and restore your purchases and the Premium entitlement.
  • To keep the App working and improve stability.

We do not sell your personal information.

Your choices

  • Tracking: change tracking permission in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, or reset your advertising identifier there.
  • Subscriptions: manage or cancel anytime in App Store → your account → Subscriptions.
  • Local data: deleting the App removes the progress stored on your device.

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Data retention

We do not hold your personal data on our own servers. Data processed by Google, RevenueCat and Apple is retained according to their respective policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the date at the top of this page.
